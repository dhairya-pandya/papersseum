How it was scored

Which games count

Every game created after uploads closed (31 Oct, 23:16 IST) in which all five bots ran their own final file: 2,579 games, about 287 per bot, from 31 Oct 23:30 IST to 3 Nov 23:15 IST. A game where any bot ran an older file is left out.

The ranking

A batch Plackett-Luce fit over the finishing order of every counted game. House bots play in those games and are part of the fit, but are not listed.

The ranges

Each strength and rank range comes from 1,000 resamples of whole games and covers the middle 95%. Strength is on an Elo-like scale centred on 1000. Where two ranges overlap, the data cannot separate the bots.

Other measures

An online rating restarted on the same games agrees on 1st and orders 2nd to 5th differently; the Plackett-Luce fit is the method published with the rules. The main site's leaderboard was frozen on 31 Oct at 23:54 IST and measures something else.
